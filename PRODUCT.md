# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> The user asked not to be interviewed ("Do not ask me to design the UI first… bạn có toàn quyền, làm đi"). Every fact below comes from their written brief; items marked *(inferred)* are assumptions drawn from it.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion, SVG/Canvas illustration, deployed on Vercel — pinned by the brief.

## Users

One person: MyMy, the author's girlfriend. She has a leg injury this year and cannot go out lantern-walking for Tết Trung Thu. She opens the link on her phone, most likely at night, sitting or lying down *(inferred)*. Secondary: LuLu (the author), who edits the copy, quiz and photos.

## Product Purpose

A 5–10 minute interactive story in which LuLu "takes" MyMy out for Trung Thu: moon intro, ticket, choosing a lantern, exploring a festival street, walking together to a bridge, opening a gift and reading a letter. Success = she feels she was actually taken out for the night.

## Positioning

A private gift, not a site. Every screen answers: "does this make MyMy feel LuLu actually took her out for Trung Thu?"

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mobile first (iPhone/Android), touch and mouse; no hover-only interactions, no keyboard controls required.
- Vietnamese copy; all messages, quiz, image paths in `/data` config.
- Lightweight on 4G; no video; audio never autoplays aggressively, with a toggle.
- Easter eggs (moon gallery, star message, fireflies) optional.

## Brand Commitments

Names "MyMy" and "LuLu". Voice: warm, playful, not overly cheesy or dramatic. Visual world pinned by brief: Vietnamese Mid-Autumn night — moon, lanterns, stars, fireflies, moon rabbit, lion dance, bridge, water reflections; cute but not childish.

## Evidence on Hand

No real photos yet; the gallery uses authored placeholders the user will replace. No music file provided — ambient sound is synthesized *(inferred decision)*.

## Product Principles

1. Story, atmosphere, personalization, interaction, animation, technical complexity — in that order.
2. Calm over flashy; the experience grows quieter and more emotional toward the end.
3. Nothing required is hard; exploration is rewarded, never gated harshly.
